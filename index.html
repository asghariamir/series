The Comparison Test: Your Safety Net

If you know one series converges or diverges, you can use it to prove what happens to another series. But there's a catch – the comparison only works one way!

📉
The Ceiling Trap
When you're smaller than something that converges, you're trapped below a ceiling. You must converge!
📈
The Rising Floor
When you're bigger than something that diverges, you're pushed up by a rising floor. You must diverge!
🤷
Bigger but Converges?
Being bigger than something small doesn't mean you're big. You might still converge!
🤔
Smaller but Diverges?
Being smaller than infinity tells you nothing. You could still diverge slowly!
